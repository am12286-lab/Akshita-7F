Red velvet Cupcake with cream cheese frosting recipe

Cup cake ingredients

  • Cake flour(sifted)
  • Baking powder
  • Salt
  • Cocoa powder (unsweetend)
  • Red food colouring
  • Butter(unsalted and softened)
  • Suger
  • 2 room temprature eggs
  • Vanilla extract
  • Buttermilk
  • White vinegar
  • Baking soda

Cream cheese frosting ingredients

  • Cream cheese not softened
  • Butter(room temprature)
  • Vanilla extract
  • Powder suger(sifted)

How to make the cup cakes

Preheat oven to 350 degrees.Line 24 cup cake papers in a muffin tin
